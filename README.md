An entry for the make.fun bounty "Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene", where entrants write a short family scene — one or two lines, warm, funny and heartfelt — that would make a proud judge smile. This entry is a small nightly ritual between siblings: a six-year-old standing guard against the monsters in the hallway, and the grown-up who still sleeps better for it.

## The entry

> Every night my little brother 'guards' the hallway so the monsters can't get me. He's six. I am twenty-four and I still wait for his all-clear before I sleep.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

The scene works because the protection runs the wrong way round: the little brother guards the adult, not the other way. The humour sits in the word "guards" and the age gap — six versus twenty-four — while the heart sits in the last clause, where the adult admits they still wait for the all-clear before sleeping. It is a whole small household in two lines, with one telling detail doing all the work: trust, handed from a child to a grown-up, and kept.
